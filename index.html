IMPORTANT INFORMATION

GENERAL EVENT GUIDELINES

  1. Each participating institution shall nominate participants within the prescribed registration deadline.
  2. A participant may take part in more than one event provided the schedules do not clash. Responsibility for managing such clashes shall rest with the participating institution.
  3. Participants must report to the respective venue at least 15 minutes before the scheduled commencement of the event.
  4. Participants shall carry a valid identity card issued by their institution.
  5. Substitution of participants after commencement of an event shall not ordinarily be permitted.
  6. Event-specific rules shall prevail wherever they differ from the general guidelines.
  7. Any misconduct, damage to property, malpractice or violation of event rules may result in disqualification.
  8. Participants shall maintain discipline and follow instructions issued by event coordinators and volunteers.
  9. The organising committee reserves the right to make minor changes to the event format where required for smooth conduct.
  10. The decision of the judges/event coordinators on matters relating to evaluation and results shall be final.
  11. If a PU college team comes to our college along with their faculty in-charge, the team will be awarded 1 additional bonus point.
